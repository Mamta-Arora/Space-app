First flight

You are parked above Earth.

Everything here is generated from one integer seed and simulated live — orbits solve Kepler's equation each frame rather than replaying an animation. Seven controls get you anywhere in the universe.

Drag Look around. Drag anywhere on the view to steer. Scroll to change distance.
Click Select an object. Click any star, planet or nebula to open its data panel and set it as your target.
W / S Travel. Throttle up and down. Hold Shift to engage warp for interstellar distances.
F Autopilot. With a target selected, fly there automatically and arrive at rest.
Space Control time. Pause and resume. Use , and . to run from real time up to a million years per second.
L Record a discovery. Save the selected object to your log. Missions track your progress automatically.
C Compare. Add up to three worlds to a side-by-side tray.
